Do More Paper Clips Make a Paper Helicopter Fall Faster?

Do more paper clips make a paper helicopter fall faster? You find out by testing it yourself: build two matching paper helicopters, time 30 drops from a fixed 1.5 m height with one, two and three clips, and compare the mean fall times and their spread. This video shows you how to run that controlled test. It doesn't give you an answer.

How steep can a ramp get before a block slides? You measure it yourself: tilt a covered board slowly, bracket the first sliding angle for paper, plastic and cloth, and use μs = tan(θ) to estimate an effective static-friction coefficient for each covering. Quick answers

What is the easiest science fair project to do the night before?

Pick a 5-minute or 1-hour project that uses kitchen supplies, like walking water or a baking soda volcano. Change one thing, measure the result three times, and chart it.

What goes on a science fair tri-fold board?

Left panel: question, hypothesis, research. Middle: big title, data charts, photos. Right: materials, procedure, results, conclusion.

How do I write a hypothesis?

Use the If, then, because format: If I change X, then Y will happen, because of a reason you can explain.
