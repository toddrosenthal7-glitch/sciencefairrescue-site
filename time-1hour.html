Which Paper Bridge Holds More Coins?

Which paper bridge holds more coins? You find out by testing it yourself: fold a flat, a doubled and an accordion bridge from the same paper, load each one with identical coins under the same conditions, and compare all nine trials. This video shows you how to run that fair test. It doesn't give you an answer.

Can a Paper Bridge Hold Coins?

Can a paper bridge hold coins, and which shape holds the most? You find out by testing it with an adult: make flat, doubled and fan-folded bridges from matching paper, add coins one at a time, and compare all nine trials. This video shows you how to run that fair test. It doesn't give you an answer.

Do More Paper Clips Make a Paper Helicopter Fall Faster?

Do more paper clips make a paper helicopter fall faster? You find out by testing it yourself: build two matching paper helicopters, time 30 drops from a fixed 1.5 m height with one, two and three clips, and compare the mean fall times and their spread. This video shows you how to run that controlled test. It doesn't give you an answer.

How Steep Before a Block Slides?

How steep can a ramp get before a block slides? You measure it yourself: tilt a covered board slowly, bracket the first sliding angle for paper, plastic and cloth, and use μs = tan(θ) to estimate an effective static-friction coefficient for each covering. This video shows you how to run that test and handle the uncertainty. It doesn't give you an answer.
