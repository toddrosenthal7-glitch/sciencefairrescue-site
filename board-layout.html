How to Lay Out Your Tri-Fold Board

Left panel

  • Question
  • Hypothesis
  • Background research

Middle panel

  • Big title on top
  • Data: charts and tables
  • Photos of the experiment

Right panel

  • Materials and procedure
  • Results
  • Conclusion

Tip: Print headings in large letters, and check your teacher's rubric first. Requirements vary by school.
