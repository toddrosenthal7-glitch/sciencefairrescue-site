Paper Airplane Design: Which Flies Farthest?

Grades 3–5Grades 6–81 Hour

Fold three designs, throw each the same way, and measure.

Question: Which paper airplane design flies the farthest?
Hypothesis: If I test three designs, then the dart design will fly farthest, because its narrow shape has less air resistance.
Safety: Never throw toward anyone's face.

Supplies (mostly already in your kitchen)

  • Printer paper (same kind for all)
  • Tape measure
  • Masking tape for a start line
  • A hallway or gym

Steps

  1. Fold three designs: a dart, a glider with wide wings, and one of your own.
  2. Mark a start line with tape.
  3. Throw each plane 5 times the same way, from the same spot.
  4. Measure each distance and write it down.
  5. Find the average for each design.

The science, explained

Four forces act on a plane: lift, weight, thrust (your throw), and drag. Wide wings create more lift but also more drag. Narrow darts have less drag but less lift. The best design balances them.
