Colored Celery: How Plants Drink Water

Grades K–2Grades 3–5Overnight

Celery stalks pull colored water up to their leaves.

Question: How does water move up through a plant?
Hypothesis: If I put celery in colored water, then the leaves will change color, because the stalk carries water up to the leaves.
Safety: Only adults use the knife.

Supplies (mostly already in your kitchen)

  • 3 celery stalks with leaves
  • 3 glasses of water
  • Food coloring
  • A knife (adult)

Steps

  1. Have an adult cut the bottom off each stalk.
  2. Add lots of food coloring to each glass (one color per glass).
  3. Put a stalk in each glass.
  4. Check after 1 hour, 4 hours, and the next morning.
  5. Have an adult cut one stalk across and look at the colored dots.

The science, explained

Plants have tubes called xylem that carry water from the roots up to the leaves. Water evaporating from the leaves pulls more water up behind it. The color comes along for the ride, showing the path.

Common question

Board tips

  • Tape a cross-section photo showing the colored xylem dots.
  • Make a timeline of photos.
  • Label a drawing: xylem, stalk, leaves.
