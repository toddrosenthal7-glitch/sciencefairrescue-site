Yeast Balloon: Does Sugar Feed Yeast?

Grades 6–81 Hour

Yeast eats sugar and blows up a balloon by itself.

Question: Does more sugar make yeast produce more gas?
Hypothesis: If I give yeast more sugar, then the balloon will inflate more, because yeast makes carbon dioxide as it eats sugar.
Safety: Warm water, not hot (hot water kills yeast). Some people are allergic to latex balloons.

Supplies (mostly already in your kitchen)

  • 3 packets of active dry yeast
  • Sugar
  • Warm water
  • 3 empty plastic bottles
  • 3 balloons
  • String and ruler

Steps

  1. Put 1 cup of warm (not hot) water and 1 packet of yeast in each bottle.
  2. Add 0, 1, and 2 tablespoons of sugar to the three bottles.
  3. Stretch a balloon over the top of each bottle.
  4. Every 15 minutes for an hour, measure around each balloon with string.
  5. Record your measurements.

The science, explained

Yeast is a tiny living fungus. When it eats sugar, it releases carbon dioxide gas, which fills the balloon. This is called fermentation, and it's the same thing that makes bread rise.

Common question

Short answer: Yeast is a tiny living fungus. When it eats sugar, it releases carbon dioxide gas, which fills the balloon. This is called fermentation, and it's the same thing that makes bread rise.

Board tips

  • Show a line graph of balloon size over time for each bottle.
  • Explain fermentation in your own words.
  • Connect it to bread.
