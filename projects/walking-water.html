Walking Water Rainbow

Grades K–2Grades 3–5Overnight

Water climbs up paper towels and mixes colors between cups.

Question: Can water travel up a paper towel and into another cup?
Hypothesis: If I connect cups of colored water with paper towels, then the water will move into the empty cups, because paper towels soak up and pull water.
Safety: Food coloring stains. Cover the table.

Supplies (mostly already in your kitchen)

  • 6 clear cups
  • Paper towels
  • Water
  • Food coloring (red, yellow, blue)

Steps

  1. Set 6 cups in a circle. Fill every other cup with water.
  2. Add red, yellow, and blue coloring to the full cups.
  3. Fold paper towels into strips and bend each one so it connects a full cup to an empty one.
  4. Check after 30 minutes, 2 hours, and the next morning. Take photos each time.

The science, explained

This is called capillary action. Water sticks to the tiny fibers in the paper towel and climbs up the gaps between them. When two colors meet in an empty cup, they mix into new colors.

  • Use time-lapse photos in a row.
  • Label which colors mixed to make orange, green, and purple.
