Skittles Rainbow: Does Water Temperature Matter?

Grades K–2Grades 3–55 Minutes

Candy color spreads in water. Test warm vs. cold.

Question: Does the color spread faster in warm water or cold water?
Hypothesis: If I use warm water, then the colors will spread faster, because the molecules in warm water move faster.
Safety: Have an adult handle hot water. Use warm, not boiling.

Supplies (mostly already in your kitchen)

  • Skittles or other coated candy
  • 2 white plates
  • Warm water and cold water
  • Timer

Steps

  1. Put a circle of candy on each plate, the same pattern on both.
  2. At the same time, pour warm water in the middle of one plate and cold water in the other.
  3. Time how long it takes the color to reach the middle.
  4. Take photos at 30 seconds, 1 minute, and 2 minutes.

The science, explained

The candy coating is sugar and color, which dissolve in water. The color spreads out by diffusion. Warm water's molecules move faster, so dissolving and spreading happen faster.
