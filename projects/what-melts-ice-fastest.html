What Melts Ice Fastest: Salt, Sugar, or Sand?

Grades 3–5Grades 6–81 Hour

Sprinkle three kitchen items on ice cubes and race them.

Question: Which melts ice fastest: salt, sugar, or sand?
Hypothesis: If I put salt, sugar, and sand on ice, then salt will melt it fastest, because salt lowers the freezing point of water the most.
Safety: Easy and safe. Don't taste the salty water.

Supplies (mostly already in your kitchen)

  • 4 ice cubes the same size
  • Table salt
  • Sugar
  • Sand or dirt
  • 4 small plates
  • Timer
  • Teaspoon

Steps

  1. Put one ice cube on each plate. One plate gets nothing (the control).
  2. Sprinkle 1 teaspoon of salt, sugar, and sand on the other three.
  3. Check every 5 minutes and note how much each cube has melted.
  4. Record the time when each cube is completely melted.

The science, explained

Salt dissolves into the thin layer of water on ice and lowers the temperature water needs to freeze, so the ice melts even below 0°C (32°F). Sugar does this too but much less, since it makes fewer particles per spoonful. Sand doesn't dissolve, so it mostly just adds a little texture.

Common question

Board tips

  • Show a line graph of time vs. how melted each cube is.
  • Explain what a control is and why you used one.
  • Connect it to why roads are salted in winter.
