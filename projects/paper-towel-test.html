Which Paper Towel Brand Absorbs the Most?

Grades 3–51 Hour

A fair test of paper towel brands with just a cup of water.

Question: Which paper towel brand soaks up the most water?
Hypothesis: If I test different brands, then the thickest brand will soak up the most water, because it has more fibers to hold water.
Safety: Ask before using scissors. Clean up spills.

Supplies (mostly already in your kitchen)

  • 3 or more paper towel brands
  • Measuring cup
  • Water
  • A bowl
  • Scissors and ruler

Steps

  1. Cut one same-size square from each brand.
  2. Put 1 cup of water in a bowl.
  3. Dip a square in for 10 seconds, lift it out, and let it drip for 10 seconds.
  4. Measure how much water is left in the bowl. The difference is how much the towel soaked up.
  5. Refill and repeat 3 times per brand.

The science, explained

Paper towels soak up water by capillary action through the spaces between their fibers. Thickness, texture, and number of layers change how much space there is to hold water.

Board tips

  • Put a bar chart of each brand's average absorption front and center.
  • Tape the actual dried squares to the board.
  • Explain why you did 3 trials (it makes the results more reliable).
